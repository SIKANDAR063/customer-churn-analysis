Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard Notes

Overview:
This dashboard provides a visual analysis of customer churn patterns using data from PostgreSQL and MongoDB. It highlights key factors influencing churn and helps identify high-risk customer segments.

Charts Explanation:

1. Churn Distribution
This chart shows the overall distribution of Active vs Churned customers.
- Total churn rate is approximately 22%.
- Indicates a significant portion of customers are leaving.

2. Churn by Company Size
This chart compares churn across different company sizes.
- Small and Large companies show higher churn (~23–24%).
- Enterprise customers have lower churn (~19%), indicating better retention.

3. Churn by Signup Source
This chart analyzes churn based on acquisition channels.
- Event-based customers have the highest churn (~26%).
- Partner-based customers have the lowest churn (~15%), indicating higher quality acquisition.

4. NPS vs Churn
This chart compares customer satisfaction (NPS) with churn.
- Even customers with high NPS are churning.
- NPS alone is not a reliable predictor of retention.

Key Insights:
- Overall churn rate is high (22%).
- Acquisition channel significantly impacts churn.
- Company size influences retention.
- Low engagement users (from MongoDB analysis) are more likely to churn.
- Behavioral data is more reliable than survey data.

Conclusion:
Combining structured data (SQL) with behavioral data (MongoDB) provides a deeper understanding of churn patterns. Improving user engagement and focusing on high-quality acquisition channels can significantly reduce churn.
